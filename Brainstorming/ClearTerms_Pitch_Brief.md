## The Idea in One Sentence

**ClearTerms is a real-time B2B trade credit underwriting API that tells sellers whether to extend credit to a buyer — before they ship the goods.**

---

## The Problem

64% of B2B sales are made on credit terms (net-30, net-60, net-90). When a supplier ships $50K of goods on net-60, they've essentially made an unsecured loan to their buyer with almost no information about whether that buyer can pay.

Current tools for this are broken in two ways:

1. **KYB (Know Your Business) checks** verify identity at onboarding — they confirm a business is real, not a fraud. They say nothing about whether that business is financially distressed *today.*
2. **Trade credit insurance** is slow, expensive, designed for large enterprises, and still relies on stale bureau data.

The result: SMB suppliers extend credit based on gut feel and payment history, and eat the losses when buyers default.

---

## The Insight

Baselayer aggregates real-time public business signals — lien filings, litigation records, officer changes, registration status, web presence — that are highly predictive of financial distress. These signals change fast. A business that stacked three short-term lender liens in the last 90 days is a very different credit risk than it was a year ago.

**ClearTerms turns those live signals into a creditworthiness score at the moment a transaction is being decided.**

This is not identity verification. It is ongoing financial health monitoring at point of sale.

---

## The Product

ClearTerms scores buyers on a 0-100 scale with an A/B/C/F rating. The score is generated by combining:

- Active lien count, lender type (equipment vs. short-term/MCA), and velocity (how many filed recently)
- Open litigation count and recency
- Registration status (active, suspended, dissolved)
- Officer/ownership changes
- Web presence quality
- Time in business

**Key design decision:** The score distinguishes between *types* of liens. Eight liens from Deere Capital, Caterpillar, and Wells Fargo on a dealership is normal floor-plan financing. Two liens from a merchant cash advance lender filed in 60 days is a distress signal.

**The demo tension:** The prototype includes a buyer — New Age Environmental Services — with a perfect KYB score (100/A, identity fully verified, 18+ years in business) but an F risk score, because they stacked 6 active liens in a 6-week window. This is the "aha" moment for the pitch: identity clean, financial risk severe. KYB would have cleared this buyer. ClearTerms would have flagged them.

---

## Go-to-Market

**Primary buyer: B2B BNPL platforms** (Resolve, TreviPay, Balance.com)

These platforms already underwrite B2B transactions at checkout. They are paying for bureau data that is stale and expensive. ClearTerms offers real-time signals via API at a lower cost per call. They are the right first customer — they already have the integration point, the use case, and the budget line.

**Business model: API — pay per call**

- Not a SaaS subscription to start
- Charged per credit check, similar to how Plaid or Stripe charges per transaction
- Low friction to trial; usage grows with customer transaction volume

**Secondary wedge (later):** Sell directly to mid-market suppliers managing large AR books — CFO/credit manager persona.

## The Key Objection to Prepare For

**"Isn't this just KYB?"**

No. KYB verifies that a business is real — that the name, tax ID, owners, and registration all check out. It is a point-in-time identity check, usually done at account opening.

ClearTerms is ongoing financial health monitoring at the moment of a credit decision. A business can pass KYB perfectly — and be stacking merchant cash advance loans, losing lawsuits, and heading toward default. Those signals show up in our score. They do not show up in any KYB product.

**Other objections to anticipate:**

- *"Bureau data already exists"* — Bureau data (D&B, Experian Business) is slow to update, expensive, and not designed for transaction-level decisions. Baselayer's signals are real-time public records.
- *"Why wouldn't BNPL platforms build this themselves?"* — They could, but they'd have to aggregate raw public records data from dozens of state sources. That's Baselayer's moat, not something a BNPL platform wants to build internally.
- *"How do you price it?"* — Per API call, consistent with how all data API businesses in this space charge (Plaid, Middesk, Persona). Volume discounts for platform customers.

1. **Hook** — "Every year, suppliers extend trillions in credit to buyers they barely know. Most of them have no idea if that buyer can actually pay."
2. **Problem** — KYB checks identity. Nothing checks ongoing creditworthiness at the moment of transaction.
3. **Insight** — Public records (liens, litigation, officer changes) are leading indicators of financial distress. They're available in real time. Nobody has turned them into a transaction-level credit signal.

6. **GTM** — B2B BNPL platforms as first customers. API model, pay per call.
7. **Market** — $460B B2B payments, growing to $1T. Trade credit insurance is a $2B market this product disrupts from the bottom.
8. **Why us / why now** — Baselayer data + summer to build + GTM motion is already mapped.
9. **Ask** — What we want from the 10-week program (build the scoring model, sign first BNPL pilot customer, validate pricing).
