Solution

Optimized B2B credit decisions for CFOs and credit managers, powered by Baselayer AI risk platform

Real-time credit risk scoring that plugs into any B2B checkout or credit approval workflow.

What we build

A credit intelligence platform that gives B2B sellers real-time visibility into buyer financial health before they extend terms.

ClearTerms continuously monitors public records (UCC filings, litigation, registration status) and surfaces risk signals the moment they appear.

Delivered as an API and dashboard that plugs into existing invoicing and ERP workflows. No new software required.
